Yes, it is free to use. AI features have daily limits to keep it available for everyone.

Curated scores are an editorial ranking based on quality, free-tier generosity and reliability. They are opinions, not user reviews.

AI can make mistakes and prices change often. Always confirm details on the tool’s own website.

Text you enter in AI features is sent to an AI service to produce the answer. Saved tools and history stay on your device. Never type passwords or sensitive personal data.
